Decoraciones Domey es un taller familiar dirigido por Tomás Jurado Quintero, un artesano y artista con décadas creando muebles y tapicería de calidad. Antes trabajaban principalmente para grandes hoteles de lujo; hoy el enfoque es claro: casas privadas de alto nivel, especialmente la comunidad expatriada de la Riviera Maya.Decoraciones Domey is a family workshop led by Tomás Jurado Quintero, a craftsman and artist with decades creating quality furniture and upholstery. They once worked mainly for large luxury hotels; today the focus is clear: high-end private homes, especially the expatriate community of the Riviera Maya.
